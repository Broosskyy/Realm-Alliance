# V1.1 P0 QA Checklist

## P02 Home
- Monster is largest interactive element.
- Gold / Spins / Shields readable without zoom.
- Wheel CTA obvious when Spins > 0.
- Daily / Quests / Heroes / Attack / Defense not visible in P0.
- No more than one primary CTA competes with tapping.

## Tap
- Entire monster is easy to hit.
- Damage feedback appears immediately.
- HP change is obvious.
- Hit FX does not cover the monster for long.

## Reward
- Normal kill reward appears for roughly 1–2 seconds or until Continue.
- Reward text is short.
- Continue is large and obvious.

## Boss
- Boss intro is short.
- Boss is visually differentiated.
- Boss defeat has larger celebration than standard monster.

## Responsive
Test at:
- 720×1600
- 1080×1920
- 1080×2400

## Renate rule
Without explanation, a first-time user should identify:
1. tap the monster
2. see the reward
3. spin the wheel
4. open village and upgrade

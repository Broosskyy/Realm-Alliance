# REALM ALLIANCE V2 — Master Mockup Convergence

The approved master mockups for **Kampf, Abenteuer, Wesen, Beute and Mehr** are binding visual and information-architecture references.

## Production rules

- Do not redesign the five primary screens away from the approved masters.
- Combat remains a fixed, non-scrolling stage with the arena as the dominant visual area.
- Adventure is world-map-first. Dungeon, tower and similar modes remain secondary.
- The persistent bottom navigation must always reserve its own layout space.
- Hero, monster, world scenery, loot, skill and VFX art remain replaceable layers.
- Production may use temporary placeholders when final art is unavailable, but placeholders must not define the long-term visual ceiling.
- Improve graphics incrementally while keeping gameplay and progression functional.
- REALM ALLIANCE V2 is a technical/architectural rebuild, not a content reset.

## Current convergence pass

Adventure now follows the master direction: Normal/Sonderwelt selector, vertically staged region journey, active/locked progression, current-region CTA, and secondary adventure modes below the main journey.

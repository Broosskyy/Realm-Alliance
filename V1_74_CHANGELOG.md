- Boss defeats now feed Village prosperity progression with a small data-driven XP link.
- Village prosperity and selected Hero mastery now feed back into active TAP strength.

- Selected Hero mastery contribution: +1.5% TAP per mastery level above 1.
- Combined Realm synergy TAP bonus is capped at +20%.
- Existing TAP momentum multiplies after the Realm synergy base, keeping the active tapping loop relevant.
- Home shows a compact REALM-KRAFT summary only when a synergy bonus exists.
- Progression overview now surfaces Realm synergy and uses the selected hero's player-facing name.
- No new navigation or gameplay buttons.

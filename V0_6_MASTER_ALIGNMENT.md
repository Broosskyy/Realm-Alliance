# V0.6 MASTER CONCEPT ALIGNMENT

Binding design interpretation for the playable source:

- Standard monsters remain short encounters.
- Bosses are highlight moments, not endurance walls.
- New content should feel new without requiring new controls.
- Village depth comes from visible progression, not freeform construction complexity.
- Heroes support idle progression without replacing the clear active Tap action.
- Wheel presentation and configured probability must agree.
- Visual progression must be visible through silhouettes, scale, buildings, regions and reward moments.
- One screen keeps one obvious primary purpose.
- Final art replacement must not require gameplay-code rewrites.

V0.6 therefore increases content variety and feedback while deliberately avoiding:
inventory grids, equipment micromanagement, hero positioning, complex village placement,
extra currencies, additional permanent bottom navigation items, and pop-up overload.

# V0.9 MASTER CONCEPT ALIGNMENT

This pass focuses on the master rule:
complexity may grow internally while player-facing interaction stays simple.

Visible UX rules:
- one obvious purpose per screen
- explicit Daily claim instead of hidden background behavior
- quests reuse actions the player already knows
- unlocks happen gradually
- rewards look consistent across modes
- no popup wall
- no additional currencies
- no inventory or equipment management
- no new permanent combat control scheme

The goal is to make Tap, Wheel, Village, Heroes, Attack and Defense feel like
one coherent game loop rather than six disconnected features.

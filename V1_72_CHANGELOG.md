# V1.72 CHANGELOG — Combat Curve & Hero Synergy

- Added an ephemeral TAP momentum system with no extra controls.
- Fast consecutive taps build up to 5 momentum tiers.
- Momentum adds up to +16% TAP damage and decays automatically after 1.15 seconds without a tap.
- Reward/Boss presentation resets momentum so encounters remain cleanly separated.
- Hero Auto-DPS now produces quiet visible assist feedback on the TAP screen.
- TAP upgrade copy reflects the current effective flowing TAP damage when momentum is active.
- Active tapping remains the primary combat interaction; no auto-play navigation was added.
- Existing hero damage authority and MonsterDefeatService transaction authority remain unchanged.

# V1.16 P0 BALANCE QA

The pass is deliberately conservative. It does not change the Master V1.4 HP growth, Wheel probability weights or Village costs.

Expected first-session behavior:
- 300 starting Gold allows a meaningful first Village choice but not all four Lv1->Lv2 upgrades.
- 5 Spins makes the Wheel immediately testable without a 50-spin early economy flood.
- Monster 1 remains exactly 10 taps at base damage 10.
- Boss 10 remains a short early highlight rather than a long wall.
- Monster rewards, Goldmine income and Wheel rewards remain unchanged in this pass.

Real-device validation is still required before calling P0 balance final. The static simulator cannot measure perceived tap fatigue, animation time or player choice behavior.
